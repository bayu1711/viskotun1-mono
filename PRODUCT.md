# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adults who follow a study-based health routine on their own, most often Achilles or other tendon rehab, sleep, nutrition or focus protocols. They open the app daily, usually on a phone, often in the middle of an exercise set or right after it. They want to know what to do today, log how it felt, and trust that the routine is safe and comes from real research.

## Product Purpose

ViskotuN1 turns peer-reviewed interventional human research into a simple daily routine. It tracks sets, adherence and discomfort, pauses progression when pain reaches the safety threshold, and answers questions from the paper's Methods section. Success means a user follows the routine correctly for its full length, logs honestly, and never gets advice the paper does not support.

## Brand Personality

Warm, honest, calm. The voice is plain and friendly, like a good physiotherapist: short sentences, no hype, no jargon on the surface. Evidence is shown as it is, including what the paper left out. The emotional goal is calm confidence: the user feels safe, not pushed.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS landing pages: rows of identical icon cards, uppercase eyebrows above every section, big-number stat blocks, gradient callouts.
- Hype health marketing: "zero hallucinations", "100% science", miracle claims, stock fitness photos unrelated to the content.
- Gamified fitness apps that pressure with streaks and badges.
- Dense clinical dashboards with tiny grey text.

## Design Principles

1. Practise what you preach: every claim on screen must be true and traceable to the paper or the policy ledger.
2. One thing at a time: one primary action per screen; supporting actions stay quiet.
3. Safety is visible: thresholds, pauses and inferred parameters are shown plainly, never buried.
4. Calm over clever: motion and color mark state changes, not decoration.
5. Readable at arm's length: large, dark body text and big tap targets for use mid-exercise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA: body text contrast at least 4.5:1, large text at least 3:1.
- Tap targets at least 44x44 px on touch screens.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all non-essential animation.
- Form inputs have linked labels and correct `autocomplete`; inputs use at least 16px text so iOS does not zoom.
- Color is never the only signal for safety state (pair with text or icon).
